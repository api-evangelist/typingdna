Remote work provides unprecedented flexibility, allowing employees to work from anywhere. However, it also introduces significant challenges for employers in accurately verifying productivity and ensuring security. Recently, devices and software called mouse movers, also known as “mouse jigglers”, have become widely used among remote workers seeking to simulate activity.

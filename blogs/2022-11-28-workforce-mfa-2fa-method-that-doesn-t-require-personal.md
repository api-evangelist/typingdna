Updated June 2025 To achieve security, enterprises work with IAM systems that deploy multi-factor authentication (MFA), or simply two-factor authentication (2FA). MFA allows businesses to verify their employees’ identities when they log in to corporate apps. While MFA is key to security, companies should avoid using methods that require employees to use their phones.
